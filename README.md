## Main Reference

**Prof. Peter Schneider — Cosmology: Lecture Notes (WS 2009/10)**

The notes cover:

- Homogeneous cosmological models and General Relativity
- FLRW cosmology and thermal history
- Gravitational lensing and cosmic shear
- Linear and non-linear structure formation
- CMB anisotropies and inflation
- Galaxy formation
